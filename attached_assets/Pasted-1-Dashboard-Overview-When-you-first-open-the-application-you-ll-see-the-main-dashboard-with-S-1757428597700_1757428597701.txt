1. Dashboard Overview
When you first open the application, you'll see the main dashboard with:

Statistics Cards: Shows total claims, verified claims, AI processing status
Recent Activity: Latest document processing and verification updates
Quick Access: Navigation to different modules
2. Document Processing & Digitization
Purpose: Convert scanned FRA documents into digital format

Navigate to "AI Processing" section
Upload scanned FRA documents (PDFs, images)
The system will:
Extract text using OCR (Optical Character Recognition)
Identify key information like village names, patta holder details, coordinates
Classify document types (IFR, CR, CFR claims)
3. Claims Management
Purpose: View and manage FRA claims data

Go to "Claims" section
View all digitized FRA claims in a table format
Filter by:
State (Madhya Pradesh, Tripura, Odisha, Telangana)
District/Village
Claim type (Individual/Community/Forest Resource Rights)
Status (Pending, Approved, Rejected)
4. WebGIS Mapping
Purpose: Visual mapping of FRA areas and assets

Access "WebGIS" section
Interactive map showing:
FRA Boundaries: Individual and community forest rights areas
Asset Layers: Agricultural land, water bodies, forest cover
Village Boundaries: Administrative boundaries
Satellite Imagery: High-resolution satellite data
Map Controls:

Zoom in/out to specific regions
Toggle different layers on/off
Click on areas to see detailed information
Search for specific villages or districts
5. Asset Detection
Purpose: AI-powered mapping of natural and social assets

Go to "Asset Detection"
The system automatically identifies:
Agricultural land and farms
Water bodies (ponds, streams, wells)
Forest cover and vegetation
Homesteads and settlements
View asset statistics and distribution maps
6. Decision Support System (DSS)
Purpose: Policy recommendations and scheme mapping

Access "Decision Support" section
The system provides:
Scheme Recommendations: Which government schemes (PM-KISAN, Jal Jeevan Mission, MGNREGA) FRA holders are eligible for
Priority Areas: Villages needing urgent intervention
Resource Allocation: Optimal distribution of development funds
Impact Analysis: Predicted outcomes of different policy decisions
7. Verification Workflow
Purpose: Systematic verification of FRA claims

Navigate to "Verification" section
Step-by-step process:
Document validation
Field verification status
Cross-reference with existing records
Approval/rejection workflow

Asset Detection is an AI-powered system that automatically identifies and maps physical infrastructure and resources in rural villages using satellite imagery. It uses computer vision and machine learning to analyze high-resolution satellite data and detect various types of assets on the ground. How it Works Satellite Data Analysis: The system processes multi-spectral satellite imagery (including visible light, near-infrared, and short-wave infrared bands) from sources like Sentinel-2 and Landsat. AI Processing: Advanced algorithms analyze spectral signatures and patterns to classify different land features and infrastructure. Asset Classification: The system categorizes detected features into specific asset types with confidence scores and precise geographic coordinates. What Assets are Detected Agricultural Assets Cropland and farmland areas Irrigation channels and farm ponds Agricultural productivity assessment Field boundaries and plot mapping Water Resources Natural water bodies (rivers, streams, ponds) Constructed water infrastructure (check dams, borewells) Seasonal water availability patterns Water stress and drought monitoring Forest Assets Forest cover classification (dense, open, degraded) Vegetation health monitoring Community forest resource areas Deforestation and regeneration tracking Built Infrastructure Homesteads and residential buildings Village settlements and habitations Roads and access paths Public facilities (schools, health centers) Key Technologies Used Spectral Indices: NDVI (vegetation), NDWI (water), NDBI (built-up areas), SAVI (soil-adjusted vegetation) Machine Learning: Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) and Random Forest classifiers Computer Vision: Automated feature detection and classification Remote Sensing: Multi-temporal satellite image analysis Output Information For each detected asset, the system provides: Precise GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude) Asset type classification (water body, agricultural land, forest, etc.) Confidence score (reliability of detection) Area measurements (size in hectares or square meters) Descriptive details (current condition, accessibility) Practical Benefits Real-time monitoring without field visits Comprehensive village asset inventory Evidence-based development planning Targeted scheme implementation Progress tracking over time Cost-effective large-scale mapping This technology enables government agencies and development organizations to make informed decisions about resource allocation, infrastructure development, and policy implementation for rural and tribal communities."
